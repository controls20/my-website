How it started

Fresh from the start.

Flax opened in 2016, when healthy food in India mostly meant a diet plan. We wanted the other thing: produce bought that morning, cooked simply, sold the same day.

Our first store opened at Breach Candy in Mumbai. Today we have cafés and cloud kitchens across Mumbai, Bengaluru and Goa, and you can order from us on Swiggy and Zomato.
